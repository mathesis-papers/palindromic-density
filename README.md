# Sharp upper-limsup density in Freeman's conjecture and an extension of the de Luca–Fischler Fibonacci formula

**Authors:** Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

For Freeman's Conjecture 7.1 (arXiv:1604.06755v2, 25 January 2017, p. 10; density in section 6, p. 8), we prove the sharp upper-limsup replacement bound 1 and extend the Fibonacci-prefix formula of de Luca, as recalled by Fischler (2006, section 1), to establish the separate Fibonacci equality for all positive integers m,r,s with r distinct from s. The density-one examples have bounded partial quotients and are not eventually periodic: increasing powers of whole prefixes force local length ratios to approach one. A block-code identity gives all qualifying Fibonacci prefixes, including the cases with a terminal digit 1 and with m=1. The upper-limsup density differs from the classical lower-liminf density.
